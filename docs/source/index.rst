How to Connect a Canon Printer to WiFi
======================================

Connecting a Canon printer to WiFi lets you print from compatible computers and mobile devices without keeping a USB cable attached. The setup method depends on your printer model: some printers have a wireless setup menu, while others use an application or a model-specific button sequence.

This guide explains the main Canon printer WiFi setup methods, how to add the connected printer to your computer, and what to check if the wireless connection fails.

Before You Start
----------------

Check that your printer includes wireless networking. Some models connect only through USB and cannot join a WiFi network.

For a wireless model, have the following ready:

* Your WiFi network name.
* Your WiFi password.
* A computer or phone connected to your home network.
* The printer's exact model name.

Switch on the printer and check its display or indicator lights for errors. Place it within a reliable signal range of the router while completing setup.

Choose the Correct Wireless Network
------------------------------------

Check your printer's wireless specifications before choosing a network. Many Canon printers use 2.4 GHz WiFi, while some models also work with 5 GHz.

Your computer and printer do not necessarily need to use the same wireless band. They need to be on the same local network with communication allowed between devices.

Avoid a guest network if it prevents devices from finding one another. Enter the WiFi password exactly, including capital letters and special characters.

Connect a Canon Printer Using Its Screen
----------------------------------------

If your printer has a display and a wireless setup menu, you can usually select the network directly from the control panel.

Open Wireless Settings
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Find the network settings or wireless LAN setup menu. The wording and menu location vary by model.

Choose the option for connecting through a wireless router. A direct connection to a phone or computer is a different connection method.

Select Your WiFi Name
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Choose your network from the available list. If several networks have similar names, check the name on your computer before continuing.

If your network is hidden, manual entry may be required where that option is available.

Enter the Password
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Type the network password carefully and confirm the settings. Wait for the printer to finish connecting.

Check for a connection confirmation. If available, view or print the network information to confirm that the printer joined the intended network.

How to Connect a Canon Printer to WiFi Without a Screen
-------------------------------------------------------

A printer without a display may use an application-assisted setup process or a particular combination of buttons.

Use the setup instructions for your exact model. Avoid copying a button sequence from a different printer, because wireless controls and indicator patterns vary.

During application-assisted setup, you may be asked to put the printer into connection mode and choose your home network. Follow the prompts until the application confirms the connection.

Some setup processes temporarily connect your device to the printer's own wireless network. Complete the remaining steps, then check that your device returns to the home network.

Canon Printer Wireless Setup Using WPS
--------------------------------------

WPS can connect a printer without manually entering the WiFi password when both the router and printer offer this feature.

Start the printer's WPS connection process using the method for your model. Then activate the router's WPS function within the required connection window.

Wait for confirmation before trying to print.

Check the router's button label carefully. A reset button can erase router settings, so do not press it in place of WPS.

If WPS is unavailable, use the printer's screen or application-assisted setup method.

Add the Wireless Printer to Windows
-----------------------------------

Once the printer has joined WiFi, add it to your computer.

On Windows 11, open Settings, choose Bluetooth & devices, and select Printers & scanners. On Windows 10, open Settings, choose Devices, and select Printers & scanners.

Choose the option to add a printer and allow the computer to search. Select your Canon printer when it appears and follow the prompts.

If additional software is required, use a package intended for your exact model and Windows version.

Open a small document, choose the newly added printer, and print a test page.

Connect a Canon Printer to a Mac Over WiFi
-----------------------------------------

Make sure your Mac and printer are connected to the same local network.

Open the Mac's printer settings and choose the option to add a printer. Select your Canon printer from the available devices.

Choose AirPrint when available for your model, or select compatible printer software.

After adding the printer, open a document and check that the correct device appears in the print dialog. Send a short test document before printing a large file.

Print from a Phone or Tablet
---------------------------

After completing Canon printer wireless setup, compatible mobile devices can print through an available printing application or built-in printing feature.

Connect your phone or tablet to the same local network as the printer. Open the document or image, choose the print option, and select the printer.

The available features depend on the printer model, mobile operating system, and application.

If the printer does not appear, check its network connection and whether your network allows devices to discover each other.

Reconnect After Changing Your Router or Password
------------------------------------------------

A printer stores the network details used during setup. Changing the WiFi name or password can leave it trying to connect with outdated information.

Run wireless setup again and enter the new network details.

If the printer continues trying to join the old network, a network settings reset may help. This removes saved connection information and requires wireless setup again.

After reconnecting, check the printer entry on your computer. An old entry may still refer to the previous network address.

Canon Printer Won't Connect to WiFi
-----------------------------------

If your Canon printer is not connecting to WiFi, identify where setup stops.

Network Does Not Appear
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Move the printer closer to the router and scan again. Check whether your model can use the network's wireless band.

If the network name is hidden, check whether your printer allows manual network entry.

Password Is Rejected
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Confirm that you selected the correct network and entered its current password.

The WiFi password may differ from the password used to manage the router. Check for typing errors before changing network settings.

Setup Connects but Computer Cannot Find the Printer
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Check that both devices are on the same local network. Guest network restrictions, device isolation, or a VPN configuration can interfere with discovery.

Confirm the printer's connection before reinstalling software.

Connection Keeps Dropping
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Check the signal near the printer. Walls, distance, and interference can make wireless communication unreliable.

If the printer stays connected but the computer reports it as offline, investigate the computer's printer entry and queue separately.

WiFi Connection and Wireless Direct
-----------------------------------

Connecting through your router places the printer on your home network.

Wireless Direct connects a compatible device directly to the printer without using that router. Depending on the device and configuration, this can affect access to your usual network or internet connection.

Choose the connection method that matches how you intend to print. For everyday printing from several home devices, connecting through the router is often convenient.

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

Can I connect a Canon printer to WiFi without a USB cable?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Many wireless models can be configured through their screen, an application, or WPS. The available method depends on the printer.

Does my Canon printer need a 2.4 GHz network?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Many models use 2.4 GHz, but wireless capabilities vary. Check your model's specifications before selecting the network.

Do I need to reconnect the printer every time I print?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Normally, the printer saves its network settings. Setup may be needed again after a router change, password change, or network settings reset.

Why is my printer connected to WiFi but not printing?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The computer may be using an incorrect printer entry, the queue may be paused, or the network may prevent device communication. Check these items after confirming the wireless connection.

How can I confirm that setup is complete?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Check the printer's network status, add it to your device, and print a small document. Successful printing confirms that the connection works for that device.

Complete Your First Wireless Print
----------------------------------

Once your Canon printer connects to WiFi, select it in your application's print dialog and send a short document.

If it prints correctly, continue with your usual files. Keep a note of the setup method used for your model so that reconnecting is easier after future network changes.
